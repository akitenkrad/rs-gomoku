# Game records and replay

rs-gomoku can replay a game from a plain-text record file (`.gmkrec`). Records can be written by hand, generated by another program, or saved from a game you played. Replay mode is also designed for screen recording: it plays back automatically at a fixed pace and can quit by itself at the end.

## Replaying a record

```sh
rs-gomoku replay examples/records/black-wins-15.gmkrec
```

The board size and rule come from the record.

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| `<FILE>` | Record file, or `-` to read from standard input |
| `--interval <INTERVAL>` | Automatic playback interval in milliseconds (default `1500`; values below the placement animation are raised to its length) |
| `--paused` | Start playback paused |
| `--exit-on-end` | Exit three seconds after reaching a finished record's end |
| `--print` | Print the moves and the final board as text instead of starting the TUI |
| `--lang <LANG>` | Display language: `en` (default) or `ja`. Also applies to `--print` |

If the file cannot be read or contains an error, rs-gomoku prints the problem (with its line number) to standard error and exits with status 1 before the TUI starts.

### Controls in replay mode

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Play / pause |
| `→` / `l` | Step forward one move (with animation) |
| `←` / `h` | Step back one move |
| `Home` / `g` | Jump to the start |
| `End` / `G` | Jump to the end |
| `+` / `-` | Faster / slower (250 ms per step) |
| `e` | Toggle the evaluation panel |
| `q` / `Esc` / `Ctrl-C` | Quit |

The status bar shows whether playback is running, the move number (`k/N`) and the current interval. When the last move ends the game, the result panel announces the winner by name or a draw; a record that stops mid-game ends with "End of record" instead.

### Text output

`--print` checks the record and prints every move, then the final board (`X` = Black, `O` = White) and the result:

```sh
rs-gomoku replay examples/records/small-9.gmkrec --print
```

```text
Move 1: Bob Black e5
Move 2: Alice White e4
...
Move 5: Bob Black d4

. . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . .
. . . X O . . . .
. . . X X O . . .
. . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . .
Result: unfinished
```

## Saving your own games

During a game or on the result screen, press `s` to save the current game as `gomoku-<unix-seconds>.gmkrec`. Files go to the current directory unless you start rs-gomoku with `--record-dir <DIR>`:

```sh
rs-gomoku --record-dir ~/gomoku-records
```

Saved records leave out the default names, so they are shown as You / CPU in English and あなた / CPU in Japanese. Add `human` / `cpu` lines to name the players.

## The `.gmkrec` format

A record is UTF-8 text: a header of `key = value` lines followed by one move per line.

```text
# rs-gomoku record v1
size  = 15         # 7..19                 (default: 15)
rule  = freestyle  # freestyle | standard  (default: freestyle)
first = human      # human | cpu           (default: human; the first player plays Black)
human = Alice      # optional display name of the "human" side (default: You / あなた)
cpu   = Bob        # optional display name of the "cpu" side   (default: CPU)

h8                 # move: column letter + row number, row 1 at the top
I9                 # case-insensitive
```

Rules:

- `#` starts a comment; blank lines are ignored.
- Header keys may only appear before the first move. Unknown or duplicated keys, and sizes outside 7–19, are errors.
- Moves alternate starting with `first`, who plays Black. See [board size and coordinates](gameplay.md#board-size-and-coordinates).
- `human` and `cpu` are just the two sides; in replay they are shown with the names you give them, so a record can describe any two players.
- A point off the board, an occupied point, or any move after the game has ended is an error. A record may stop before the game ends.

Sample records in [`examples/records/`](../examples/records/):

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `black-wins-15.gmkrec` | 15 × 15, freestyle: Black wins with a four-three |
| `small-9.gmkrec` | A short game on a 9 × 9 board |
| `overline-standard.gmkrec` | 9 × 9, standard rule: an overline is made and the game goes on |
| `unfinished.gmkrec` | A record that stops mid-game |

## Recording a video

Replay mode makes a game reproducible, so you can record the same game as often as you like. Make sure the recording terminal is large enough for the board (see [terminal requirements](gameplay.md#terminal-requirements)).

With [asciinema](https://asciinema.org):

```sh
asciinema rec -c "rs-gomoku replay game.gmkrec --exit-on-end" gomoku.cast
```

With [VHS](https://github.com/charmbracelet/vhs), a tape file such as:

```text
Output gomoku.gif
Set Width 1400
Set Height 800
Type "rs-gomoku replay game.gmkrec --interval 1000 --exit-on-end"
Enter
Sleep 30s
```

`--exit-on-end` leaves the final board and result on screen for three seconds and then quits, so the recording ends cleanly. Use `--paused` instead if you want to step through the game by hand while recording.
